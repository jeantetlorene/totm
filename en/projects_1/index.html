Current Projects

Coordinating Nesting Activity Monitoring in Guadeloupe

Partners: Guadeloupe Sea Turtle Network

Coordinated monitoring of marine turtle nesting activity across the Guadeloupe archipelago.

Defining a Methodology for Selecting Identification Techniques

Partners: Reunion Regional Museums Network (Kelonia, Sea Turtle Observatory)

Building a shared framework to choose the most relevant sea turtle identification methods.

Impact of IUU Fishing on Leatherback Turtle Population Dynamics in French Guiana

Partners: CNRS, WWF French Guiana

Studying how illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing affects marine turtle populations in French Guiana.

Developing a Data Collection Protocol for IUU Fishing in French Guiana

Partners: Amana Nature Reserve

Standardizing illegal fishing data collection to better monitor pressures on turtles and habitats.

Coordinating the French West Indies National Action Plan for Marine Turtles

Coordinating implementation of the national marine turtle action plan across the French West Indies.

Developing Methods to Assess Pain and Stress in Marine Turtles

Partners: Reunion Regional Museums Network (Kelonia, Sea Turtle Observatory), University of Valencia

Improving the clinical assessment of pain and stress in marine turtles.

Developing a Practical Guide to Blood Sampling in Marine Turtles

Partners: Reunion Regional Museums Network (Kelonia, Sea Turtle Observatory)

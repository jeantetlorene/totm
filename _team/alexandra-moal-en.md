For more than 15 years, her life choices revolving around the conservation of sea turtles in Guadeloupe have given her extensive experience in the non-profit sector: fieldwork, teamwork, event organization, stakeholder network coordination, fundraising, and training in nest monitoring, photo-identification, and feeding-area population surveys.

She also creates her own 3D sea turtle educational models using recycled materials, and now works as an artist as well.

To further expand her contribution, she founded the communication studio Missocom, where she developed numerous outreach, informational, and educational materials. Scientific communication and targeted writing, combined with coherent graphic design, are her strengths for producing clear, accurate, and engaging materials that are well tailored to the intended audience.

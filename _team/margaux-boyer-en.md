Margaux, an agronomist specializing in environmental sciences, has extensive experience in overseas territories, having lived and worked in French Polynesia and Guadeloupe. Before joining TOTM, she worked on marine turtle projects in Malaysia, mainland France, and Guadeloupe. Since then, she has undertaken missions in French Guiana, Réunion Island, and Mayotte. Her diverse career has allowed her to contribute to the conservation of wildlife and natural habitats across various contexts: research, non-profit organizations, consulting firms, and public institutions.

With her international experience, she has also contributed to sustainable fisheries and ocean conservation programs, developing valuable skills in environmental project management, multi-stakeholder coordination, and fundraising. At TOTM, she now leads the development of the association, overseeing fundraising, implementing conservation actions, designing new projects, and networking stakeholders involved in marine turtle protection at both national and international levels.

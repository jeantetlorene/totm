## Our History

Trans Océans Tortues Marines (TOTM) is a non-profit association under the French Law of 1901 that brings together stakeholders from French territories to conserve marine turtles worldwide. It was founded in 2019 by these same stakeholders in order to implement recommendations that had been formulated over several years. Its legal status enables it to seek and manage the funding required to carry out these unifying national actions for marine turtle conservation.

## Our Missions

<ul> 
    <li>Promoting, studying, and conserving marine turtles and their habitats,</li>
    <li>Implementing actions in support of marine turtles based on priorities identified by scientific and technical groups specializing in marine turtle conservation. Action methods are selected by the association’s members and validated by the Board of Directors,</li>
    <li>Supporting the implementation of cross-cutting actions at international (International Union for Conservation of Nature, International Sea Turtle Society, State of the World Sea Turtles…), inter-regional (OSPAR, Barcelona Convention…), national (Groupe Tortues Marines France, Société Herpétologique de France…), or supranational levels (Mediterranean Conference on Marine Turtles, Wider Caribbean Sea Turtle Conservation Network – WIDECAST, Memorandum of Understanding on the Conservation and Management of Marine Turtles and their Habitats of the Indian Ocean and South-East Asia – IOSEA…). These actions address marine turtles, their habitats, and/or the threats they face in French territories. This support may take various forms, including scientific, technical, logistical, and financial assistance,</li>
    <li>Producing and disseminating educational materials and tools for the general public, schools, and young people.</li>
</ul>

## How We Operate

TOTM places governance at the heart of its operations, guided by a commitment to transparent decision-making. Since 2022, TOTM’s governance has been structured around working committees, each composed of a Chair and a Vice-Chair, appointed by the Board of Directors following a voluntary application process.

Committee Chairs regularly report on their work to the association’s Executive Board. All materials and meeting minutes are made available to board members.

The committees are organized into two divisions: an “Actions” division, dedicated to TOTM’s projects, and a “Support and Development” division, focused on the structuring and strategic development of the association.

Since early 2024, the association has been supported by a full-time Project Officer, who is involved in all of the association’s projects.

This project focused on improving how pain and stress are characterized in marine turtles, especially in care and rehabilitation settings.

The work began with a literature review covering acute stress, chronic stress and ways to assess pain in sea turtles.

## Objectives

- Identify the most relevant parameters for clinical monitoring.
- Better inform analgesic use and care practices.
- Open up new applied research directions.

## Follow-up

The project laid the scientific groundwork for future collaborations and new monitoring protocols.

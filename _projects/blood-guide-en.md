This project was designed to produce a practical French-language guide to blood sampling and interpretation in marine turtles.

Blood analyses are increasingly valuable for assessing turtle physiology, but they require specialized interpretation that bridges veterinary practice and sea turtle-specific knowledge.

## Objectives

- Gather the available literature on marine turtle hematology and biochemistry.
- Produce a useful support document for veterinarians, caregivers and care centers.
- Improve how clinical and scientific blood data are interpreted and used.

## Usefulness

The guide is intended to improve marine turtle care while strengthening knowledge sharing between professionals.

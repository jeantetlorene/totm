This project evaluated the effects of voluntary Turtle Excluder Device (TED) adoption by the French Guianese shrimp trawler fleet.

The analysis used several onboard observer programs and long-term extrapolation of bycatch data.

## Objectives

- Quantify changes in accidental capture after TED adoption.
- Document a concrete European example of improved fishing practices.
- Support ongoing discussions about wider TED requirements in import supply chains.

## Contribution

The findings provide strong preliminary evidence that TEDs can deliver measurable conservation gains and strengthen the case for broader uptake.

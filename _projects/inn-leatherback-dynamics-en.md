This study is part of the TOTM Initiatives action focused on strengthening the fight against illegal fishing.

Illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing is a major threat in French Guiana. It operates close to nesting sites and greatly increases the risk of accidental turtle capture.

## Objectives

- Study the interaction between IUU fishing and leatherback and green turtle populations.
- Map marine risk areas in order to guide conservation priorities.
- Produce analyses and maps that can support institutions and local management partners.

## Why it matters

The project is designed to help identify priority areas for surveillance and inform regional discussions on marine turtle protection.
